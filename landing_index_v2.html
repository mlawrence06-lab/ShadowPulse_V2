What is MELT?

The MELT Method® is a revolutionary self-care system designed to help you get out and stay out of chronic pain, improve performance, and eliminate the stress of active living and aging.

Using specialized soft rollers and small balls, MELT rehydrates the connective tissue, rebalances the nervous system, and restores space to compressed joints.
