Client Meeting Checklist

1. Online Store (WooCommerce)

Goal: Determine what they are selling and how.

  • Products:
    • What are we selling? (Physical items like rollers, or digital downloads?)
    • Do images and descriptions already exist, or do we need to create them?
    • Are there variations? (e.g., Sizes, Colors)
  • Payments:
    • How do you want to get paid? (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)
    • Do you have these accounts set up already?
  • Shipping:
    • Do you ship physically?
    • If yes, is it Flat Rate, Free Shipping over $X, or calculated by weight?
    • Do you offer local pickup?
  • Taxes:
    • Do we need to charge GST/HST?

2. Appointments (Amelia)

Goal: Configure their booking schedule and services.

  • Services:
    • What specific appointments can people book? (e.g., "1 Hour Assessment", "30 Min Consultation")
    • What is the price and duration for each?
    • Do you need "buffer time" between appointments to clean up or prepare?
  • Staff:
    • Is it just you, or are there other instructors?
    • Does each person have their own Google Calendar to sync with?
  • Availability:
    • What are your "Business Hours"? (e.g., Mon-Fri 9-5)
    • Are there specific days off?
  • Notifications:
    • Do you want SMS reminders for clients? (Use Twilio or similar service - additional cost?)
    • Customized email confirmation text? (e.g., "Please wear comfortable clothing").

3. Email Marketing (MailPoet)

Goal: Set up their communication channel.

  • Lists:
    • Do you have an existing list of emails to import?
  • Content:
    • What do you plan to send? (Monthly Newsletters, New Product Alerts, Tips & Tricks?)
  • Frequency:
    • How often? (Weekly, Monthly, Ad-hoc?)
  • Automation:
    • Do you want a "Welcome Email" series to go out automatically when someone signs up?

4. Rewards / Gamification (GamiPress)

Goal: Define how users are rewarded for engagement.

  • Points:
    • What actions earn points? (Logging in daily, commenting on blog posts, buying a product?)
    • What is the exchange rate? (e.g., 100 points = $1 discount?)
  • Ranks:
    • Do you want "Levels"? (e.g., Beginner -> Advanced -> Master)
    • What perks come with higher ranks?
  • Badges/Achievements:
    • Do you want fun badges? (e.g., "First Purchase", "Early Bird Booker")

5. Design & Brand

Goal: Ensure the site looks and feels right.

  • Visuals:
    • Do we have a Brand Kit? (Logo files, Hex color codes, Fonts)
    • "MELT Soft Roller" graphic: I have created a placeholder. Do you have a professional photo to replace it?
  • Tone:
    • How should the site "speak"? (Professional/Clinical vs. Warm/Friendly vs. High-Energy)
